help_outline How it works

Langton's ant walks on a grid. On a cell of state i it repaints the cell to state i+1 (wrapping), turns according to the new state's arrow, then steps forward. Complex highways and chaos emerge from tiny rules like LR.

2D turns: L left · R right · U U-turn · C continue straight.

3D bee: flies on a cubic lattice with a heading + an "up". Turns: Forward · Back · Left · Right · Up · Down (relative to the bee).

Effects: the 2D view is a custom WebGL shader — cell glow, ant light, grid and vignette are toggleable in the Effects card. The 3D view uses exponential fog plus a headlight that follows the lead bee.

Rules: presets are documented turmites — the 1986 LR highway ant, symmetric LLRR, chaotic RLR, and the 2024 infinite-highway family. 3D presets follow Hamann's 2003 three-dimensional ant definition.

Engine: flat Uint8Array grids, branch-free inner loop, batched steps per animation frame, WebGL instancing — no per-step allocation, no DOM churn.
